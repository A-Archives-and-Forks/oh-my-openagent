1. The checked-in categorized audit is red on committed `HEAD` because it scans its own allowlist.

4. Final alias stdout differs beyond the allowed old-name -> new-name normalization.
5. Required aggregate `task-6.txt`, final Codex/doctor transcript, full-gate transcript, lazycodex transformed-package baseline, and post-merge publish-run evidence are absent.
6. The required documented `omo-ai = senpi + packages/*senpi*` end-state composition is missing.
